Why we're building this

Deals start messy. A buyer emails a purchase order on Tuesday. The price is in one thread, the payment terms are in last month's quote, and the delivery date is in a spreadsheet only one person can find.

Somebody has to pull that together, check it, chase the missing piece and remember to follow up. In most companies that somebody is your best salesperson, at seven in the evening.

See how it works

Doing business is hard enough. The work around it shouldn't be.

02Handoffs

03Repeats

  • Trade

    Every deal, where it stands

    A list of your open deals with what's done, what's stuck and who it's waiting on. It does the job of the tracking spreadsheet.

  • Intelligence

    Reads what you send it

    Emails, PDFs, a note typed after a call. It pulls out the buyer, the goods, the price, the terms and the dates, and flags what disagrees. Every item links back to the line it came from.

  • Network

    Connect once, trade with many

    Your buyers, suppliers, forwarders and banks, each with their history. Your own company file, the Trade Passport, holds your registration and documents, so you share them once and stop emailing them every time.

  • Finance

    Make every trade finance-ready

    Terms, due dates and settlement live in the deal. If you want funding, the request goes to your bank or funder with the paperwork attached. TRAIBOX doesn't lend, and no money moves without you.

  • Clearance

    Checks and proof

    Identity checks, sanctions screening, export rules, sustainability reporting. TRAIBOX requests them from the providers you pick and files each result with the deal, so the proof is already there when someone asks.

    Questions

    Questions we get asked.

    What is TRAIBOX?

    Software for companies that import and export. You give it an order and it works out what the deal needs, does the chasing and gets the paperwork ready. You approve anything that goes out.

    Does it replace my ERP or my bank?

    No. It connects to them. Your ERP keeps the orders and your bank keeps the money. TRAIBOX does the work in between.

    Who approves what?

    You do. TRAIBOX drafts, checks and chases. Anything that commits your business, like sending terms to a buyer or requesting a payment, waits for your yes.

    Is TRAIBOX a bank or a broker?

    Neither. We don't lend, and we don't sit between you and your bank. We get the deal ready so that asking your bank or funder takes less back and forth.

    When can I use it?

    Not yet. We're planning a private beta for January 2027. If you have a real deal you'd like to run through it, ask for early access.
