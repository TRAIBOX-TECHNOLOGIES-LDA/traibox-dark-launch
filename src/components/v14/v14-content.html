    Questions

    Frequently asked questions.

    What is TRAIBOX?

    TRAIBOX is an agentic trade engine designed for businesses buying and selling goods and services. It brings the details of a deal together, helps identify what is missing and coordinates the next steps.

    Does it replace my ERP or my bank?

    No. TRAIBOX is designed to work with supported systems and financial providers. Your existing records and services stay where they belong.

    Who approves what?

    Your team sets the permissions and retains control of consequential actions. The sample walkthrough shows a human approval before a proposed request.

    Is TRAIBOX a bank or a broker?

    TRAIBOX is not a lender. It is designed to help prepare and coordinate payment and funding requests through relevant providers.
